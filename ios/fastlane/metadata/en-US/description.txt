A reader that shows Japanese and English text one word at a time (one phrase at a time for Japanese) in the same place on the screen, a style known as RSVP. You can read on at the speed you choose without moving your eyes from side to side.

What you can do
- Paste the text you want to read, or open a text file on your device (including Aozora Bunko txt files)
- Read English one word at a time and Japanese one phrase at a time
- Set the speed in words per minute for English and characters per minute for Japanese
- Get a slightly longer pause at commas, at the end of sentences, and at the end of paragraphs
- Pause to see the sentences around your place and the word you are on
- Pick up where you left off: the app remembers your place for each text

Your text stays on your device
Splitting and playback happen entirely on this device. The app never sends your text to a server, and the developer never receives or stores it. The text itself is not saved on your device either.

Please note
The app's screens are in Japanese. Word and phrase splitting may contain mistakes. The app does not promise faster reading or better comprehension. E-books with DRM cannot be opened.
